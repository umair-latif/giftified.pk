# 23 — Customer-shared templates

**Branch:** `feat/shared-templates` · **Suggested owner:** any AI assistant (lead reviews) ·
**Needs:** 19, 22

Signed-in customers tap **Share as template** on a saved design. All their photos are removed and
replaced by sample photos from the founder's placeholder library (matched to frame shape); text stays
editable. Submissions go to a review queue; the founder approves or rejects (simple admin page).
Approved templates show "Design by <first name>" in the gallery. No customer photo is ever public.
