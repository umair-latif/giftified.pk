# Mockup photo brief (send to the print vendor / studio)

Goal: product photos that the preview can print on realistically, the way
Printful or Redbubble mockups look. One photo per product, colour and view.

## Shot list (launch)

| #   | Product | Colour | View  | Style                               |
| --- | ------- | ------ | ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1   | T-shirt | White  | Front | Ghost mannequin (or clean flat-lay) |
| 2   | T-shirt | Black  | Front | same set-up as #1                   |
| 3   | Hoodie  | Grey   | Front | same set-up as #1 (hood down)       |

Later (up to 3 colours per garment): repeat the same set-up for each new colour.
Add a back view only when printing on the back is offered.

## Rules for every shot

- **Same set-up every time**: same camera position, height, distance, lens, light and
  garment placement for all colours of a product. Use a tripod; do not move it between colours.
- **Camera**: straight on, lens at chest height, centred. No wide-angle distortion
  (use 50-85 mm equivalent, shoot from 2-3 m).
- **Size**: at least 2000 x 2000 px (square), garment filling ~75% of the frame. JPEG at
  maximum quality or PNG. Do not sharpen, filter or apply "beauty" retouching.
- **Light**: soft, even, from the front-left (about 45 degrees) plus a weak fill on the right.
  Visible, natural folds are good; harsh shadows and blown-out whites are not. No coloured light.
- **Background**: plain neutral (mid grey or white) paper or cloth, evenly lit, no props.
- **Garment**: freshly steamed, collar shaped, sleeves symmetrical, hem straight. Hoodie: hood
  down, drawstrings straight, pocket seam visible. Real blank from the vendor (the one
  customers will receive).
- **Reference**: in the first frame of each set-up, also shoot a grey card (18% grey) and a
  ruler or a 300 x 400 mm rectangle taped flat on the chest, so we know the real print position and scale.
- **A second frame per colour**: the same shot with a 300 x 400 mm placeholder (flat colour) printed
  or taped where the print goes. We trace the print outline and folds from it.

## File naming

`tshirt-white-front.jpg`, `tshirt-black-front.jpg`, `hoodie-grey-front.jpg`;
placeholder frames: `tshirt-white-front-placeholder.jpg`, and so on. Reference/grey card:
`tshirt-white-front-reference.jpg`.

## Questions for the vendor

- Print position: distance from the collar seam to the top of the print (mm), and the maximum print area per product.
- Exact garment sizes/measurements for the photographed blank (width at chest).
- Permission to use the photos on giftified.pk.
